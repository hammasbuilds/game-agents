one update

  • 12 environmentsstepped in parallel processes

what the update actually does

  • r = π(a|s) / π_old(a|s)how much more likely the new policy makes the action it took
  • A = Σ (γλ)ᵏ δadvantage by GAE, γ 0.99, λ 0.95 — how much better that action was than expected
  • L = −min(rA, clip(r, 0.9, 1.1)·A)the clip is what stops one update moving the policy too far
  • + ½(V − R)² − 0.01·Hfit the value head, and pay the policy to stay uncertain a while longer
