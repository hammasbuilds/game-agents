Input: a pictureThe game renders at full size. Nothing is simplified for the agent's benefit.
84×84, greyscaleColour and detail are dropped. The film keeps the full render; the agent never sees it.
Four frames stackedMotion has to be visible in a single observation, or the policy is guessing direction.
ConvolutionsThree of them, then a hidden layer: ~428k parameters, small enough to train on a laptop core.
Policy and valueOne head chooses the button, the other estimates what the position is worth.
Twelve games in parallel128 steps each, 1,536 transitions per update, advantages by GAE.
PPOClipped surrogate, four epochs, entropy bonus. The whole update is in one readable file.
The ladderSix checkpoints kept, including the network before a single gradient step.
Output: a verdictBest checkpoint chosen on one set of episodes, reported on another, and only called a win if it clears two standard errors.
